Private Lessons

Experience focused, distraction-free instruction in a dedicated learning environment designed to help students build confidence and master new skills. My studio lessons provide access to all necessary materials and technology, creating an ideal setting for personalized, hands-on learning.

Group Lessons

Group lessons are a fun and collaborative way to learn alongside friends, family, or fellow musicians. Each one-hour session accommodates up to four participants and is tailored to the group's skill level and goals. Students learn together in an engaging and supportive environment.

Virtual Lesson

Learn from anywhere with flexible online instruction tailored to your schedule. Virtual lessons offer the same personalized guidance and one-on-one attention as in-person sessions, making it easy to stay on track without the need to travel.

In-Home Lesson

Enjoy the convenience of professional instruction delivered directly to your home. In-home lessons allow students to learn in a familiar, comfortable environment while receiving individualized support and coaching tailored to their specific goals.
